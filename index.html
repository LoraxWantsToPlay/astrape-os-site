01 / Philosophy

Software should answer to you.

A line in the sand. Astrape starts with a simple conviction: the systems we rely on should be understandable, adaptable and under our control.

Local-first by design

Designed for locally hosted AI, with the freedom to select the services and hardware you trust.

Modularity over lock-in

Replaceable components and configuration-driven choices instead of a single prescribed stack.

Privacy through choice

Choose where processing happens. Local and external providers have different privacy implications; the choice belongs to you.

02 / The public foundation

Astrape Core.

The open-source foundation for modular AI orchestration. The initial public release explores natural voice interaction, speech integration and configurable inference.

Core is an early-stage project, not a finished consumer product. Explore the source, experiment with the existing voice loop and contribute to the public foundation.
